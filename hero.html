My hero is Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo is a famous Portuguese professional soccer player widely considered one of the best athletes in the history of the sport

Club History
He has played for major teams like Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, and Al-Nassr.
National Team
He captains the Portugal national team and holds records for the most international goals and appearances.
Trophies
He has won 35 major trophies, including five UEFA Champions League titles.
